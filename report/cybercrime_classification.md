
# Cybercrime Classification and Modelling

## 1. Introduction

Operation Phantom Swipe is a simulated investigation involving ATM skimming, credit card cloning and online financial fraud. The investigation focuses on identifying cybercrime activities and classifying them according to their nature and impact.

## 2. Classification of Cybercrimes

### A. ATM Skimming
ATM skimming involves the use of unauthorized devices to capture card information during ATM transactions.

Classification:
- Financial Cybercrime
- Data Theft
- Unauthorized Data Collection

### B. Credit Card Cloning
Credit card cloning involves copying stolen card information onto another card or payment medium for fraudulent use.

Classification:
- Identity Theft
- Financial Fraud
- Payment Card Fraud

### C. Online Credit Card Fraud
Online credit card fraud involves using stolen payment information to conduct unauthorized online transactions.

Classification:
- Online Financial Fraud
- Identity Theft
- Computer-Related Fraud

## 5. International Convention: Budapest Convention

Relevant provisions include:

| Article | Description |
|---|---|
| Article 2 | Illegal access |
| Article 3 | Illegal interception |
| Article 4 | Data interference |
| Article 7 | Computer-related forgery |
| Article 8 | Computer-related fraud |
| Article 14 | Scope of procedural provisions |
| Article 23 | International cooperation |

India is not a party to the Budapest Convention. Its provisions are used here as an international reference framework for comparing cybercrime laws and cooperation mechanisms.

## 6. Cybercrime Taxonomy

The crimes can be classified into the following categories:

1. Financial Crimes: Unauthorized transactions and financial theft.
2. Identity Crimes: Misuse of stolen card information.
3. Data Crimes: Unauthorized collection and copying of financial information.
4. Computer-Related Crimes: Fraud involving digital devices and online platforms.
5. Cross-Border Crimes: Fraudulent activities involving multiple countries.

## 7. Impact of Cybercrime

- Financial losses to cardholders and banks.
- Misuse of personal and financial information.
- Loss of trust in electronic payment systems.
- Challenges in identifying criminals operating across borders.

## 8. Conclusion

Operation Phantom Swipe demonstrates how multiple cybercrime techniques can be combined to conduct financial fraud. Proper classification of crimes and legal mapping help investigators understand the nature of offences and identify relevant legal provisions.

The investigation also highlights the importance of international cooperation, digital evidence preservation and effective cybersecurity practices.
